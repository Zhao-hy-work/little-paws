A little planning, a kinder home

Every animal needs daily care, even on your busiest days. Use this guide to plan before you bring a pet home.

Everyday care

  • Provide fresh water and food suitable for the species.
  • Check appetite, behaviour and the living area each day.
  • Plan cleaning, enrichment, exercise and rest around the animal's needs.

Plan for the long term

  • Budget for housing, food, supplies and routine and emergency veterinary care.
  • Check your household's agreement, housing rules and possible allergies.
  • Choose a reliable carer for holidays, illness and busy periods.

Ask a vet experienced with your chosen species about health questions. If an animal stops eating or suddenly behaves differently, seek professional advice promptly.
