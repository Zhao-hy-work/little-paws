Basic pet care

A pet needs care every day, not just when we want to play.

A few things to remember

  • Provide fresh water and food suitable for the animal.
  • Keep the living area safe and clean.
  • Make time for exercise, rest and gentle attention.

Each animal has its own needs. Ask a vet or a trusted animal-care organisation for detailed advice.
