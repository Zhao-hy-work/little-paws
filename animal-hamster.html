Hamsters

A small evening explorer for someone who likes quietly observing busy animals.

Before bringing hamster home

A large, well-designed enclosure, deep suitable bedding and an appropriately sized wheel. Let it sleep during the day. Syrian hamsters must live alone; wheel and digging sounds may disturb sleep.

Individual animals differ. Check the full needs of your chosen species with a reliable rescue or an experienced vet.
