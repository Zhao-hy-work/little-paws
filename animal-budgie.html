Budgies

Lively birds for someone who enjoys sound, patient training and social interaction.

Before bringing budgie home

Suitable bird companionship, a spacious flight cage and safe daily flight time, enrichment and an avian vet. Budgies are vocal; fumes, unsafe windows and other household hazards need attention.

Individual animals differ. Check the full needs of your chosen species with a reliable rescue or an experienced vet.
