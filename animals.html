Meet the animals

Every pet has its own habits and needs. Get to know these four animal friends.

🐱

Cats

Cats are curious animals. Many enjoy climbing, playing and resting in quiet places.

  • Activity: Chasing a toy during a short play session.
  • Care tip: Provide a scratching post and a quiet place to hide.
🐶

Dogs

Dogs need daily exercise, patient training and time with the people they trust.

  • Activity: Exploring new smells on a walk.
  • Care tip: Match exercise to the dog's age, health and needs.
🐰

Rabbits

Rabbits are social animals who need plenty of space to hop, stretch and explore.

  • Activity: Exploring safe tunnels and hiding places.
  • Care tip: Provide plenty of hay and fresh water every day.
🐹

Hamsters

Hamsters often become active in the evening and at night. They enjoy digging and exploring.

  • Activity: Making tunnels in deep, suitable bedding.
  • Care tip: Let a sleeping hamster rest and handle it gently when awake.
